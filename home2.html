Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before starting your immigration journey.

How long does the visa process take?
Processing times vary by visa type and country. Generally, work visas take 3–8 weeks, student visas 2–6 weeks, and family visas 6–12 weeks. During your free consultation, we'll give you a personalized timeline based on your specific case and target country.
What documents do I need for my application?
Required documents depend on your visa type and destination country, but typically include: valid passport, proof of funds, educational credentials, employment letters, language test scores, and medical certificates. We provide a personalized checklist and review every document before submission.
Can my family accompany me?
In most cases, yes. Many work and study visas allow dependents (spouse and children under 18) to accompany the primary applicant. Specific rules vary by country and visa category. We'll advise you on the best approach for your family's situation during your consultation.
What if my visa application is rejected?
While our 98% success rate means rejections are rare, if your application is refused, we'll analyze the refusal letter, identify the issues, and advise on the best path forward — whether that's an appeal, administrative review, or a fresh application addressing the concerns.
How much do your services cost?
Our fees depend on the complexity of your case and visa type. We offer transparent, fixed-fee pricing with no hidden charges. Your initial consultation is completely free, and we'll provide a detailed quote before you commit to anything.
