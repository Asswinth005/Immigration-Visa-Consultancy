Our Story

From a Small Office to Global Reach

Horizon Visa was founded in 2010 by a group of immigration lawyers and former visa officers who saw firsthand how confusing and stressful the immigration process could be. We believed there had to be a better way — one built on transparency, expertise, and genuine care for each client's unique story.

What started as a two-person office in Austin, Texas, has grown into a global consultancy with advisors across three continents. But our core philosophy remains unchanged: every client deserves a personalized strategy, honest guidance, and unwavering support throughout their journey.

Today, we've helped over 12,000 individuals and families start new chapters in 45+ countries — and we're just getting started.

Our Mission

To empower individuals and families to achieve their global dreams by providing expert, ethical, and accessible immigration guidance — turning complex processes into clear, confident pathways to new horizons.
